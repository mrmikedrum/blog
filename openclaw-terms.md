Effective October 13, 2026.

## Personal-use integration

[OpenClaw Calendar]({{ '/openclaw/' | relative_url }}) is operated by Mike Drum for personal scheduling assistance. It is not offered as a public service, and these pages do not grant anyone access to the assistant or to a Google account. There are no subscription fees for this personal integration.

## Authorization and permitted use

Only accounts and calendars that the operator is authorized to access may be connected. Google OAuth permissions and each calendar’s sharing settings govern access. Users must comply with applicable Google terms and must not use the integration to access or change another person’s data without authorization.

## Calendar actions and accuracy

The assistant may read schedules and make requested changes where write access is available. AI-generated responses can be inaccurate, and changes to shared events can affect other people or send notifications. The operator is responsible for reviewing important dates, recipients, and requested changes. The integration is not intended as the sole source for emergency or safety-critical scheduling.

## Availability and ending access

The integration is provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation or accurate results. It may be changed or discontinued. The account holder may disconnect it through [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) at any time. Disconnecting does not undo existing calendar changes.

## Privacy and contact

See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/openclaw/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for information about data processing, retention, and deletion requests. Contact [mike@drum.zone](mailto:mike@drum.zone) with questions.
