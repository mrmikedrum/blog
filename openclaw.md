OpenClaw Calendar is a personal integration operated by Mike Drum. It connects an authorized Google account to a privately operated OpenClaw assistant for reviewing schedules, preparing combined agendas, and creating or updating calendar events when requested.

The integration can use the primary calendar and calendars shared with the connected account. Each calendar’s existing Google sharing permissions still apply: read-only calendars cannot be edited.

This integration is for the operator’s personal use. It is not a public sign-up service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. This public information page does not provide access to the assistant or to calendar data.

## Data and permissions

Google Calendar access is granted through Google’s OAuth consent screen. The integration requests Calendar read/write access and basic account identity information to identify the connected account. Calendar content may be processed by the AI service configured in OpenClaw to fulfill the operator’s requests.
